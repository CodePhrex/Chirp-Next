# Chirp! design system
The two original PNGs are the sole master visual authority. Preserve original bytes under assets/reference/; never replace them with generated approximations. Artwork upload remains pending.
## Tokens
Abyss Black #020711; Midnight Navy #04152C; Electric Blue #006CFF; Liquid Cyan #00D9FF; Glacier Blue #9BDFFF; Platinum White #F2F7FF.
Use dark navy surfaces, restrained luminous edges, high-contrast text, dimensional liquid-glass reflections. Preserve the supplied metallic wordmark; do not substitute a generic type treatment for the official asset.
## Interaction
The orb is the primary touch target. Minimum 48dp interactive controls; support TalkBack, explicit status text, reduced motion, adjustable haptics and non-color status cues. Never indicate transmission until permission and transport readiness are confirmed. Release, cancellation and lifecycle interruption stop capture.
## Visual acceptance
Compare idle silhouette, specular highlights, refraction, edge illumination and composition against both original images on physical Android hardware. Review all nine states and transitions, light/dark surroundings, small screens and reduced-motion mode. Record screenshots, device model, frame timing and reviewer outcome. Target 60 FPS where supported; adaptive quality must preserve identity. No placeholder may pass final visual acceptance.
