# Approved Chirp! blueprint
Approved November 25, 2026. Product: Chirp! by CodePhrex. Tagline: Instantly Connected.
## Identity
Original reference artwork is authoritative. Liquid glass, dimensional depth, luminous energy, metallic wordmark. Palette: #020711, #04152C, #006CFF, #00D9FF, #9BDFFF, #F2F7FF.
## Modules
Command Center, Orb Engine, Chirp! Live, Direct Chirps, Channels, Contacts, Discovery, Recents, Presence Engine, Orb Studio, Sound Studio, Haptic Engine, Notification Center, Profile Center, Privacy Center, Chirp! Store, Subscription Center, Administration.
## Architecture
Native Android Kotlin, Jetpack Compose, OpenGL ES/GLSL procedural mesh deformation, WebRTC/Opus, Firebase Auth/FCM, Kotlin or TypeScript backend, PostgreSQL, Redis, WebSockets, Room, GitHub Actions. Future iOS, desktop, web.
## Collections
Water (default), Inferno, Nebula, Hypercube, Royal, Obsidian. Each may have unique physics, sound and haptics.
## Commerce
Free: direct Chirps subject to reasonable use, water orb, basic channels. Plus: premium collections, advanced customization, custom sounds and haptics. Elite: exclusive legendary orbs and advanced channel management. Pricing TBD.
## Roadmap
1. Foundation and brand
2. Signature Orb Engine
3. Communication engine
4. Application experience
5. Social and channels
6. Customization and commerce
7. Production readiness
## Quality gates
Reference fidelity, consistent premium design, smooth transitions, accessible non-color state indicators, configurable haptics, physical device testing, documented decisions and regression tests. Target 60 FPS on supported devices with adaptive quality. No static placeholder orb accepted. Preserve legacy repository.
